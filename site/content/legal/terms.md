## WizeLens Cloud at app.wizelens.com

- **Plans and prices.** The plans are Free, Team, Business, and Enterprise. Prices, included data, and retention are on the
  [pricing page](/pricing/). Enterprise is set with us by hand.
- **Paying.** Team and Business are paid through Stripe, monthly or yearly. On Team and Business, data past the included GB
  keeps coming in and is billed each month on a usage invoice. Owners can set a spend cap; past it, the cloud stops taking
  data until the next month or a higher cap.
- **Free.** The Free plan stops taking data at 10 GB in a month, until the 1st or an upgrade.
- **Retention.** Telemetry is kept for your project's retention, up to your plan's limit. When you move to a plan with a
  shorter limit, retention drops to that limit, and older data is removed.
- **Payments that fail.** If a payment fails, the plan stays while Stripe retries. Then the organization moves to Free.
- **Cancelling.** Owners and admins cancel under Billing, Manage billing. You can then delete the organization, which deletes
  its projects, their telemetry, and its audit log.
- **Your data.** The data your apps send is yours. You are responsible for having the right to send it.
- **Fair use.** Please do not use the cloud to break the law, to attack or probe other people's systems, to get around plan
  limits, or to slow the cloud for others. We may suspend an account that does.
